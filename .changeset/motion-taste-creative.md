Creative components: Scene (a 2D/3D stage the model composes from shapes, glyphs, text, images and lines with per-item depth and motion), Animate (entrance, loop or pointer motion for any node), Orbit (cards on a 3D ring) and Layers (an exploded 3D stack). Restyled away from generic generated-UI tells: no gradient text or purple glows, one accent fill (`--ag-motion-fill` replaces `--ag-motion-grad`), neutral HoloCard foils (default `silver`), single-color Ranking bars, theme-colored confetti, sentence-case labels.
